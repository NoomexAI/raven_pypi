When a battery cell temperature exceeds 45°C during a standard charge cycle, the EPS master controller must immediately command a charge current reduction to 50% capacity. If the temperature continuously climbs and crosses the critical threshold of 55°C, an automated solid-state switch must disconnect the primary battery bus within 15 milliseconds to prevent thermal runaway. Under these conditions, the spacecraft will transition entirely to solar array direct-energy regulation mode. If the main bus voltage drops below 24.2V for a consecutive period exceeding 8 seconds, the EPS telemetry module triggers a Level 1 Load Shedding event. This event automatically cuts power to all non-essential payloads, including experimental sensors and secondary transmitters. If the voltage fails to stabilize and degrades past 22.0V, a Level 2 Emergency Shedding protocol is initiated, forcing the onboard computer into Safe Mode and reserving remaining power exclusively for life support, thermal survival heaters, and primary telemetry command links.
